    Rules that shape the math

    Expiry
    Blocks and FLEX expire on the last active day of each semester. Nothing rolls over, not even to spring.
    Daily cap
    At most 4 blocks a day and 2 per meal period. If your required pace is above 4 a day, some blocks cannot be saved.
    Meal periods
    Breakfast 3:30 to 10:29 AM · Lunch 10:30 AM to 4:29 PM · Dinner 4:30 to 8:59 PM · Late night 9:00 PM to 3:29 AM.
    Changing plans
    Fall only, by 5 PM on Sep 18, 2026, through the StarRez dining portal. There is no spring change window.
    Guest meals
    Traditional plans include two guest meals a semester on top of your blocks.
